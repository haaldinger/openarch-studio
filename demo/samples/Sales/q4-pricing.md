# Q4 Pricing Guide (Sales, internal)

Price list and discount guidance for Northwind Labs deals signed in the fourth quarter.

## List prices

All prices are per year, billed annually, in euros.

| Plan | List price | Included users |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | €6,000 | 10 |
| Team | €18,000 | 50 |
| Enterprise | €60,000 | 250 |

Additional Enterprise users cost €200 each per year. Premium support adds 12% to the plan price.

## Discount policy

Representatives may offer discounts up to the limits below without approval.

- Starter and Team: up to 10%.
- Enterprise: discount floor: 18%. Enterprise deals may not be discounted by more than 18% without written approval from the VP of Sales, so the lowest Enterprise price a rep may quote is €49,200.

Multi-year commitments may add 5% for two years and 8% for three years, which count toward the approval limit.

## Approvals

Requests beyond the limits go to the VP of Sales with the customer name, deal size and the competing offer. Approvals are normally answered within one business day.

## Quarter-end

Pricing for new deals does not change in December. Contracts must be signed by 31 December to count toward Q4.
