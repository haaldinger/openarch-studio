# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

Welcome to Northwind Labs. This handbook summarises how we work together. It applies to every employee and contractor.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Outside core hours, teams agree their own schedules. Please record planned absences in the shared calendar at least one week ahead.

## Time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid leave per year, plus public holidays. Unused leave of up to five days may be carried into the next year. Sick leave is paid in full for the first ten days of any illness; please tell your manager as early as you can.

## Expenses and travel

Book travel through the company portal. Economy class applies to flights under six hours. Submit expense reports within 30 days, with receipts attached.

## Compensation and bonuses

Northwind Labs pays an annual bonus to eligible employees. Bonus details are restricted to HR and Finance. If you have questions about your own bonus, contact your HR partner directly rather than asking colleagues.

## Equipment and security

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and a screen lock of five minutes or less. Report lost devices to IT within one hour. Never share passwords, and use the company password manager for shared credentials.

## Conduct

We treat each other with respect. Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with your manager, HR, or the confidential ethics line.

## Questions

For anything not covered here, ask your manager or email people@northwind.example.
