# 2027 Bonus Policy (HR, confidential)

This policy explains how annual bonuses are calculated at Northwind Labs for the 2027 performance year.

## How the pool is sized

The bonus pool is 8% of base salary, summed across all eligible employees. Finance confirms the total amount each year; HR applies the rules below to individual awards.

## Eligibility

- Employees must have completed 6 months of service by 31 December 2027 to be eligible.
- Employees must be employed and not under notice on the payment date.
- Contractors and interns are not eligible.
- Part-time employees receive a pro-rated bonus based on contracted hours.

## Calculation

An individual bonus equals base salary multiplied by the target percentage for the role, adjusted by the performance rating:

| Rating | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Exceeds expectations | 1.25 |
| Meets expectations | 1.00 |
| Partially meets expectations | 0.50 |
| Does not meet expectations | 0 |

Employees who join during the year are pro-rated from their start date.

## Payment

Bonuses are paid with the March 2028 payroll. Payments are taxed as regular income.

## Confidentiality

Bonus amounts are confidential. Employees must not share individual awards. Managers receive only the figures for their own direct reports. Questions go to your HR partner.
