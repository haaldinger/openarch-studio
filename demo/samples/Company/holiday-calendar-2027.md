# Northwind Labs Holiday Calendar 2027

The offices are closed on the dates below. Teams that provide on-call support arrange cover in advance with their manager.

| Date | Holiday |
|---|---|
| 1 January | New Year's Day |
| 26 March | Good Friday |

| 1 May | Labour Day |
| 6 May | Ascension Day |
| 17 May | Whit Monday |
| 3 October | Day of German Unity |
| 25 December | Christmas Day |
| 26 December | Boxing Day |

## Company shutdown

Northwind Labs closes between Christmas and New Year. The shutdown runs from 27 December to 31 December 2027 and does not count against annual leave. Support engineers on the emergency rota receive a day in lieu for each day worked.

## Summer Friday

From the first Friday in July to the last Friday in August, the offices close at 13:00 on Fridays. Please plan customer meetings accordingly.

## Booking leave

Add your leave to the shared calendar at least one week ahead. Requests longer than ten consecutive working days need approval from your manager and should be made at least a month in advance.

If a public holiday falls on a weekend, no substitute day is granted.
