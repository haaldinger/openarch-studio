# Salary Bands 2027 (HR, confidential)

These are the annual base salary bands for Northwind Labs, in euros, for the 2027 pay cycle. They are reviewed once a year by HR and approved by the executive team.

## Engineering

| Level | Title | Band minimum | Band maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Junior Engineer | €48,000 | €62,000 |
| E2 | Engineer | €60,000 | €78,000 |
| E3 | Senior Engineer | €76,000 | €98,000 |
| E4 | Staff Engineer | €94,000 | €122,000 |
| E5 | Principal Engineer | €118,000 | €150,000 |

## Product and Design

| Level | Title | Band minimum | Band maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| P2 | Product Manager | €62,000 | €80,000 |
| P3 | Senior Product Manager | €78,000 | €102,000 |
| D3 | Senior Designer | €70,000 | €92,000 |

## How bands are used

New hires are normally offered between the minimum and the midpoint of the band. Moving above the midpoint requires approval from the HR director. Promotions move an employee into the next band, and the new salary must reach at least the new minimum.

## Confidentiality

Salary bands are for HR and approving managers only. Please do not forward this document.
