# Q3 Revenue Forecast (Finance, confidential)

Prepared by the Northwind Labs Finance team after the July close. Figures are in euros and are forecasts, not results.

## Headline numbers

| Metric | Forecast | vs. plan |
|---|---|---|
| Q3 revenue | €6.8M | +2% |
| Recurring revenue | €5.5M | +3% |
| Services revenue | €1.3M | -2% |
| Operating margin | 14% | flat |

## Drivers

Recurring revenue is ahead of plan because three Enterprise renewals closed early and expansion in the Nordic region was stronger than expected. Services revenue is slightly behind because two implementation projects slipped into Q4.

## Risks

- Two large renewals, together worth about €400,000 a year, have not yet been signed.
- Currency movements could reduce reported revenue by up to 1% if the dollar weakens further.
- Hiring in Customer Success is behind plan, which may delay onboarding of new accounts.

## Cash

Closing cash for Q3 is forecast at €9.4M. Collections remain healthy, with average days sales outstanding at 41 days.

## Next steps

Finance will refresh this forecast after the mid-quarter pipeline review and share the update with the executive team. Questions go to the Finance planning mailbox.
