# 2027 Bonus Budget (Finance, confidential)

Prepared by the Northwind Labs Finance team for the 2027 planning cycle. Distribution: Finance and HR leadership only.

## Summary

The 2027 bonus pool: €1.2M. This is an increase of 6% on the 2026 pool of €1.13M and is fully accrued in the 2027 operating budget.

## Allocation by area

| Area | Share | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering | 45% | €540,000 |
| Sales | 25% | €300,000 |
| Operations | 15% | €180,000 |
| Customer Success | 10% | €120,000 |
| Corporate functions | 5% | €60,000 |

## Funding assumptions

The pool is funded on the assumption that 2027 revenue reaches at least 95% of plan. If revenue lands below 90% of plan, Finance will recommend reducing the pool by up to 30%. The CFO approves any change to the pool.

## Payment timing

Bonuses are paid with the March 2028 payroll, after the audited year-end results are confirmed. Accruals are reviewed quarterly, and Finance reports the accrual status to the executive team each quarter.

## Related documents

The eligibility rules and the calculation method are set out in the HR bonus policy for 2027. Finance does not decide individual awards.
