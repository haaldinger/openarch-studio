# 2027 Sales Commission Plan (Sales, confidential)

This plan applies to quota-carrying Northwind Labs sales representatives for the 2027 fiscal year.

## Base commission rates

| Deal type | Commission rate |
|---|---|
| New business | 10% of first-year contract value |
| Renewal | 3% of annual contract value |
| Expansion | 6% of incremental annual contract value |
| Services | 2% of services revenue |

## Accelerators

Once a representative passes 100% of the annual quota, the rate on every further deal rises by half: new business pays 15%. Above 150% of quota the accelerator doubles the base rate, so new business pays 20%. Accelerators apply only to deals booked after the threshold is crossed.

## Quota and attainment

Annual quotas are set per territory by Sales leadership in January. Attainment is calculated on signed contracts with a start date in 2027.

## Payment

Commission is paid monthly in arrears, in the payroll after the deal is booked and the first invoice is issued. Deals with unpaid invoices after 90 days are held back.

## Clawback

If a customer cancels within 90 days of signing, the commission paid on that deal is recovered from the next payment.

## Disputes

Raise questions about attributions with your sales manager within 30 days of the payment.
